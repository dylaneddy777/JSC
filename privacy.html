Johnson Sign Financial Reporting Integration

Privacy Policy

Effective date: August 7, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Johnson Sign Company (“Johnson Sign,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) handles information through the Johnson Sign Financial Reporting Integration (the “Integration”). The Integration is a private, internal business tool used only by authorized Johnson Sign personnel. It is not offered to the public.

1. Information the Integration accesses

With authorization from Johnson Sign’s QuickBooks Online administrator, the Integration may access accounting and business records held in Johnson Sign’s QuickBooks Online account. Depending on the reports and features used, this information may include:

  • chart-of-accounts and general-ledger information;
  • customers, vendors, products, services, classes, departments, and projects;
  • invoices, bills, payments, purchases, deposits, journal entries, and related transaction details;
  • financial reports, balances, and aging information;
  • business contact information contained in authorized accounting records; and
  • technical information needed to operate and protect the Integration, such as authorization metadata, timestamps, processing logs, and error records.

The Integration is intended to retrieve information for internal reporting. It is not intended to modify Johnson Sign’s QuickBooks Online accounting records.

2. How information is used

Information accessed through the Integration is used only for legitimate Johnson Sign business purposes, including:

  • internal accounting, financial analysis, and management reporting;
  • Power BI dashboards and related business intelligence;
  • reconciliation, data-quality review, forecasting, and operational analysis;
  • maintaining, securing, troubleshooting, and auditing the Integration; and
  • meeting legal, accounting, tax, and recordkeeping obligations.

3. Sharing and disclosure

We do not sell information obtained through the Integration, use it for targeted advertising, or make it available to unaffiliated third parties for their own marketing purposes.

Information may be available to authorized Johnson Sign personnel and to service providers that support hosting, databases, security, analytics, or other technical operations. Such providers may process information only to provide services to Johnson Sign and are subject to applicable contractual and confidentiality obligations. We may also disclose information when required by law, legal process, or a valid governmental request, or when reasonably necessary to protect rights, security, or the integrity of our systems.

4. Data security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and organizational safeguards appropriate to the nature of the information. These safeguards may include access restrictions, secure credential storage, encrypted network connections, logging, and separation of reporting access from QuickBooks administration. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

5. Retention and deletion

We retain information only as long as reasonably necessary for the purposes described above, including accounting, audit, backup, security, and legal requirements. Information may remain for a limited period in protected backups or logs after it is removed from active reporting systems.

6. Authorization and disconnection

An authorized QuickBooks Online administrator may review or revoke the Integration’s access through the connected-app settings available in QuickBooks Online. Johnson Sign may also disable the Integration or remove its stored authorization credentials.

7. Your choices and inquiries

If you believe information relating to you is present in Johnson Sign’s authorized accounting records, or if you have a question about access, correction, retention, or deletion, contact us using the information below. Requests may be subject to identity verification and applicable legal or accounting obligations.

8. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy as the Integration, our practices, or applicable requirements change. The effective date at the top of the policy identifies the latest revision.
